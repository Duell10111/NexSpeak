# Friends

Like on Discord, a direct message alone doesn't make someone your friend: you can message people
without being friends. Friends are listed under **Friends** at the top of **Direct Messages** (the
first button in the server list), which is also what you see when you open it.

## Adding friends

1. Open **Friends** and click **Add friend**.
2. Search by name or enter a full Matrix ID like `@alice:matrix.org`. People you don't share a room
   with can only be found by their full Matrix ID.
3. Click **Send friend request**.

You can also add someone from their profile: click them in the member list and choose
**Add friend**.

The other person gets the request under **Pending** (the number next to **Friends** and on the
Direct Messages button counts incoming requests). They can accept or decline it. Until then the
request is listed as an outgoing friend request on your side, where you can cancel it.

## The friends list

- **Online** shows friends who are online or away, **All** shows every friend, **Pending** shows
  incoming and outgoing requests. **Online** is missing if your homeserver doesn't share presence.
- Each friend has buttons to send a **message** or start a **call**; both open your direct
  message with them.
- **More** offers **Remove friend** and **Ignore**. Removing ends the friendship for both of you;
  your direct messages stay. Ignoring also removes the friend and hides their messages, invites and
  friend requests (see [Safety](chat.md#safety)).

The dot on a friend's avatar and on direct messages in the sidebar shows whether they are online
(green), away (yellow) or offline (grey). It is missing if your homeserver doesn't share presence.

## Compatibility with Element

Friendship is stored in your direct message with that person, so it is the same on all your
devices. Element and other Matrix apps show the conversation but not the friendship. A friend
request to someone you have no direct message with yet arrives as an invite there.
